# Jurassic Domination 0.1.0

First playable Windows build. Offline, single player, keyboard and mouse.

- Start as an egg and hatch into one of four randomized beginner species.
- Discover 35 species across eight tiers: 27 dinosaurs and eight mythological creatures.
- Choose from three next forms and three mutations at each evolution. Eight stackable mutation types change your build; the final tier continues through increasingly difficult ascensions.
- Aim your bite, trigger one of six species ability types, dodge through danger, eat defeated prey and manage hunger and stamina.
- Explore four increasingly dangerous radial regions, watch for enemy attack telegraphs and survive timed alpha hunts.
- Earn ancestral DNA after death, improve three permanent lineage upgrades and record discoveries, achievements, recent runs and personal bests.
- Includes title screen, tutorial, field guide, pause menu, settings and death/retry flow.
- Original animated procedural 3D creatures, vegetation, skeletal remains, crystals, water, particles, impact arcs and lighting.
- Original adaptive synthesized music, jungle ambience and combat effects, with independent volume controls.
- Three difficulty modes, three graphics quality settings, fullscreen, reduced motion support, optional shake and damage numbers, automatic pause on focus loss.
- Sandboxed Electron Windows build with local assets and local saves. No runtime network connection is required.

The game is a commercial-development foundation. Controller support, control remapping, localization, wider hardware coverage, Steamworks features, signing and external playtesting remain future release work. Runs do not suspend across application exits; persistent DNA and records do. The title illustration is AI generated; actual gameplay uses code-authored 3D models.
